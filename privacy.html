Privacy Policy

HA Inventory Sync is a personal application used solely by its owner.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Rclone to access the owner's Google Drive for synchronizing Home Assistant inventory and backup files. Its Google Drive permission allows reading, creating, modifying and deleting files.

Data Handling

Files are transferred between the owner's Home Assistant installation and Google Drive. File contents are not published on this website or sold to third parties.

Access Revocation

The owner may revoke access through Google Account security settings.
